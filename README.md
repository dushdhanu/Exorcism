# Yaka: Seal of the Graveyard (Concept)

**Working Title:** Yaka: The Cursed Hour (or Graveyard Shift: Yaksha Protocol)  
**Genre:** Third-person Action RPG / Supernatural Arena Brawler  
**Target Atmosphere & Setting:** Misty colonial and ancient Sri Lankan graveyard (Sohena) overgrown with banyan roots, crumbled stone tombs, flickering oil torches, clay fire bowls (pandam), and thick fog glowing with sinister blue-green spiritual wisps.

## 1. Core Premise & Narrative

Two siblings—playable as either the young boy or girl—unearth an ancient talismanic bracer (Yantra Bandhana) buried beneath an ancient graveyard altar. The artifact works like an elemental relic dial, granting the bearer the power to channel and transform into distinct ancient ancestral warrior-avatars (spirit champions).

The boundary between realms has shattered, unleashing Sri Lanka’s most feared graveyard spirits and demons (Yakkas). The protagonists must adapt their forms to exploit each spirit’s weaknesses before the graveyard is entirely consumed.

## 2. Transformation Dial & Tactical Matchups

Players activate their wrist relic to transform in real time. Each form matches or counters specific spirit behaviors:

* **Sun-Flame Avatar (Suriya Divya) vs. Mohini:**
  * **Enemy Behavior:** Mohini lures players through spectral fog, phantom crying babies, and illusionary clones that sap sanity.
  * **Counter:** The Flame Avatar unleashes blinding solar light bursts that banish illusions and expose Mohini's true skeletal form.

* **Earth-Breaker Titan (Maha Gaja) vs. Mahasona:**
  * **Enemy Behavior:** Mahasona stalks through graves with his black hound, delivering crushing club attacks and fear-inducing roars.
  * **Counter:** High-poise, blunt-force transformation capable of parrying heavy blows, destroying tombstones for projectiles, and breaking Mahasona’s guard.

* **Gale-Blade Hunter (Vayu Yodheya) vs. Kalu Kumaraya:**
  * **Enemy Behavior:** Kalu Kumaraya uses shadow-stepping, mind-clouding miasma, and fast dagger strikes from the dark.
  * **Counter:** Hyper-mobile air dashes and wind-cleaving daggers that dispel shadow patches and pin down rapid teleports.

* **Ritual Cleanser (Shanthikarma Mystic) vs. Giri Yaka & Gara Yaka:**
  * **Enemy Behavior:** Afflicts the player with status curses, terrain poison, and frantic mask attacks.
  * **Counter:** Uses sacred ash (vibhuti), protective barrier mantras, and counter-curses to turn demonic debuffs back onto them.

## 3. Key Gameplay Features

* **Relic Transformation System (Dial-Swap):** Real-time transformation wheel with an energy meter (Prana). Swapping mid-combo allows fluid aerial-to-ground chain attacks.
* **"Desperation Climax" (Grave Convergence):** At designated wave peaks or low health thresholds, all lingering ghosts converge in a chaotic multi-boss assault. Players must rapidly cycle through multiple transformations mid-fight—using the wind form to dodge Kalu Kumaraya while using solar pulses to keep Mohini at bay and heavy armor to tank Mahasona’s ground slams.
* **Interactive Graveyard Environment:** Breakable crypts, ancient bell towers to ring for stun waves, sacred bo-tree shrines for recharging stamina, and mud pits that slow down movement.
